Industrial Excellence

A Premier Architectural System House in Libya

AinZara functions as a complete "System House" and high-tech glass processing plant. We combine high-precision aluminum profile extrusions with custom-engineered architectural solutions designed for Mediterranean solar irradiance, wind loads, and thermal insulation demands.

From high-rise commercial curtain walls in Tripoli to luxury residential developments, our engineering department provides comprehensive shop drawings, structural wind-load simulations, and fabrication to exact millimeter tolerances.

Quality Assurance

Tested & Certified Performance
